Chilli Pan Mee


Pork topping works beautifully as a handpie filling: add a little sauted cabbage and chopped green onion, plus a tiny smear of chilli condiment. 20 minutes at 400f using basic pastry crust.

Pork Topping:

150 grams minced pork
1 tablespoon soy sauce
1 teaspoon dark soy sauce
1 1/2 teaspoons oyster sauce
1/4 teaspoon pepper
1/2 teaspoon sugar
===
1 shallot, sliced
1 garlic clove, minced
2 tablespoons oil
===
Half-cooked egg (5-6 minute boil;1 per portion)
Green onion
Fried shallot
leafy greens
side soup


Marinate pork in sauces, pepper and sugar for at least 30 minutes. Saute shallot and garlic in oil until golden and soft. Add pork and cook till done.

Top a small portion of round, chinese wheat noodles with a couple tablespoons seasoned meat, a half cooked egg, fried shallots and green onion. Serve with chilli condiment, leafy greens (baby lettuce or steamed bok choy) and a simple kelp side soup.

Chilli Condiment:

2 tbsp fresh red chilli or bell pepper (for volume, not heat)
15 g dried chilli pepper, coarsely ground
12-15 grams dried shrimp, finely chopped, ground or pounded
12 grams garlic, minced
1/8 teaspoon sugar
1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
2 1/2 tablespoons oil


Saute dried shrimp in oil until fragrant. Add the chillies, and other ingrediants; cook until dry and a darker shade. Use as spicy condiment.
