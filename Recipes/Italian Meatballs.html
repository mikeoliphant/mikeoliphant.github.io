Italian Meatballs




3 c day-old bread cubes
1 1/4 pounds ground beef
3 eggs, beaten
3 garlic cloves, minced
3/4 c grated pecorino
1/4 cup italian parsley, minced
1/4 c pine nuts
1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
1/2 teaspoon black pepper
1/4 cup olive oil
2 1/2 c mario sauce


Toast the pine nuts for 8 minutes in a 400f oven.
Soak bread cubes in water for a couple minutes, then squeeze out excess moisture.
Combine bread cubes, beef, eggs, garlic pecorino, parsley, pine nuts, salt and pepper. Use wet hands to shape into golf-ball sized meatballs.
Heat oil in heavy skillet; brown meatballs on all sides (about 10 minutes). Add tomato sauce and simmer 30 minutes.
