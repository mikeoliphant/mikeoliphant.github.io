Rice Wine


Fruity and somewhat sweet; similar to mirin. Mostly used for cooking. The liquid will clear over time, but the cloudy portion at the bottom is also useable.

4 rice cups sweet rice (japanese)
1 rice yeast ball


Cook the rice normally. Spread onto a sheet pan to cool.
Crush the yeast ball into a fine powder using a mortar and pestle.
Place about an inch of rice into a large glass jar and sprinkle with some of the yeast. Repeat as needed untill all rice and yeast are used.
Cover, but leave slightly open. (eg jar with screw top lid, slightly loose.)
Allow to ferment about 14 days at room temp.
Strain the mixture through cheesecloth. Refrigerate to store. Transfer to a bottle once cleared if desired.
