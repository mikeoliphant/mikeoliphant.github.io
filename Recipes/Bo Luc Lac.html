Bo Luc Lac


Super tasty. I have increased the sauce amounts for the cooked meat. Recipe includes pineapple, but we omit it.

2 tablespoons fresh lime juice
1 1/2 tablespoons fish sauce
4 teaspoons sugar
2-3 red, thai chilies, chopped
1 tablespoon oyster sauce
2 teaspoons soy sauce
2/3 pound beef steak (eg rib eye or new york strip)
2-3 tablespoons oil
2 large garlic cloves, sliced
1/2 cup asian basil leaves, torn if large
1/4 red onion, thinly sliced lengthwise
1 ripe tomato, cut in wedges


In a large-ish bowl, mix the lime juice, fish sauce, sugar and chiles. Stir to dissolve the sugar and set aside.
Mix the oyster sauce and soy sauce together in a medium bowl. Cut steak into 1/2 cubes and coat with the oyster/soy sauce mixture.
Heat the oil in a large fry pan until almost smoking. Add the garlic slices, stir once and add the beef cubes. Let sit 30 seconds then stirfry until the meat is slightly charred and just cooked (about 2 min total).
Transfer meat to the bowl with the lime juice dressing and toss. Deglaze the pan with a couple tablespoons of water and add to the meat along with the basil and onion strips.
Spread the watercress and tomatoes onto individual dinner plates and top with a generous portion of meat and several spoonfuls of sauce. Serve with a banh mi style baguette along side.
