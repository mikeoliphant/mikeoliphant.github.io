Red Beer


Make the mix in advance and cool it down before mixing with the beer.

Serves 2:

6 1/2 oz canned tomato with juice
1/8 teaspoon black pepper
1/8 teaspoon garlic powder
1/8 t onion powder
pinch msg
1 teaspoon red Hot Sauce
1/2 t habanero hot sauce
2 teaspoons fish sauce
2 pacifico beers


In a blender, mix everything except beers. Chill.
Pour mixture into two glasses. Carefully pour a beer into each one, stirring gently to avoid foaming over.
Enjoy!
