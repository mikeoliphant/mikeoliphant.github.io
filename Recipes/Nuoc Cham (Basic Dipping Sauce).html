Nuoc Cham (Basic Dipping Sauce)


Update: Use equal parts lime, fish sauce, sugar and half part water. Chopped garlic and chile are important.

Original: Use more water for basic dipping sauce, medium water for salad dressing and the smallest amount for Banh Cuon dipping sauce. Makes about 2/3 cup - plenty for two people. Taste and adjust with additional fish sauce and/or sugar if lime is overly tart.

Original:

20-40 ml lime juice
20-40 ml fish sauce
20-40 grams sugar
10-20 ml water
1-2 minced or smashed garlic clove
1/2-1 chopped, red thai chile


Original:

39 ml lime juice (1.33 oz or 2.75 tbls)
39 ml fish sauce (1.33 oz or 2.75 tbls)
39-78 ml water (78 = 2.67 oz or >5 tbls)
1 1/2 teaspoons rice wine vinegar
20 grams sugar (1.5 tbls)
1 minced garlic clove (optional)
1 minced thai chile (optional)
