Chickpeas (Instant Pot)


Makes enough for one batch of hummus, plus some extra for snacking.
Cooking time and water amount from Amy + Jacky: pressurecookrecipes.com
They have an unsoaked version as well, but I haven't tried it.

1/2 cup (112 grams) dried chickpeas
1/4 baking soda
1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
2 cups water (plus soaking water)
3/4 teaspoon kosher salt


Add the baking soda and 1/2 salt to about 2 cups water; soak chickpeas overnight. Drain well.
Add 2 cups water, 3/4 t k salt and chickpeas to pressure cooker.
Use high pressure for 5 minutes with natural release. Texture: remove from cooker after 10 minutes for firmer; let sit 30 minutes for very soft.
