Pizza dough - homemade


The 2025 version makes two pizzas plus a bread loaf. For the bread, use dough 2 - 7 days later; add about 1/4 flour and some water the day before baking and let warm/rise at room temp several hours. Bake at 400 about 35 minutes.

2012 - use the last of Mike's rustic bread dough. Very wet, spreadable dough; use plenty of olive oil. Gets nice and thin.

2019 - 2025 (current) - Very Reliable:

575 g AP Flour 370-400 g water (less in summer, more in winter) 1 T k salt 1 1/3 t instant yeast


Mix, let sit a bit, mix and put in container
Let rise at RT a couple of hours
Overnight in fridge
Remove from fridge ~2 hours before baking time
280 g / pizza
450 degree oven
