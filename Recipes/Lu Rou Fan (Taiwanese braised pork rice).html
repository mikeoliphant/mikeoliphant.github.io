Lu Rou Fan (Taiwanese braised pork rice)


Half and half pork belly and pork shoulder works as well or better than just belly.

Instant Pot version is good, just be sure to start with about 1 cup liquid and condense a few minutes afterward. (22min HP + 16 min NR)

1 1/4 pounds pork belly, skin on
1/4 pound pork shoulder
1/4 cup soy sauce (+1 tbsp for ipot)
1/4 cup rice wine (homemade) (+1 tbsp for IPot)
1 star anise
1 (approx. 2 inch) cinnamon stick
2 garlic cloves, sliced or smashed
1 (1 inch) piece ginger, peeled and sliced
1/4 cup water (+2 tbsp for ipot)


Cut pork into large chunks. Place in small sauce pan with remaining ingredients. Cover and braise gently 2 to 2 1/2 hours, turning occasionally (OR Instant Pot 22min + 15min NatRelease). When pork is tender, but not falling apart, remove from heat and let cool. (IPot: simmer sauce 10 minutes to condense).
Refrigerate, and then cut pork into thick batons and return to sauce. Serve warm over rice or in a bao (steamed bun).
