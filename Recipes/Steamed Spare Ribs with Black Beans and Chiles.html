Steamed Spare Ribs with Black Beans and Chiles


I often used about 1.25-1.5 pounds of ribs. Really good as a noodle soup topping.
Pressure cooker: 25 minutes with 10 minute natural release

Ribs:

1 lb spare ribs, cut into bite-sized chunks
1 tablespoon soy sauce
1 tablespoon shaoxing wine
1 teaspoon minced ginger
1 tbsp fermented black beans, rinsed
2 teaspoons lard
1 large pinch of chile flakes


Blanch ribs a minute or two in boiling water. Drain and rinse off any extra protein stuff.
Place ribs in a bowl just large enough to hold them. add the soy sauce, shaoxing wine, and ginger. Mix well. Top with the black beans, chile flakes and lard.
Steam ribs over high heat for about an hour. Stir part way through and add more steaming water if needed. Serve over steamed rice or as a noodle topping.

Noodles for 2:

2.5 - 3 cups stock (or 2c stock, 1c water and 1 t chicken boullion)
2 teaspoons soy sauce
4 ounce chinese round noodles
Sliced green onion
chile oil condiment
asian greens (eg bok choy)
