Roast Pork


2025 updated the recipe from loin to shoulder.

2 1/2 pounds pork shoulder roast
3-4 cloves garlic, sliced thickly
2 teaspoons kosher salt
Black pepper
1 large onion


Pre-heat oven to 425* F.
Cut slits into pork and insert garlic slices every few inches.
Rub a generous amount of salt and black pepper all over the pork roast. Let sit at room temp, about 1 hour (optional).
Place onion wedges under meat, add about 1/2 cup water and roast for 20-25 minutes (or place directly on oven rack with pan beneath). Reduce heat to 350 and cook another 60-80 minutes (internal temp anywhere from 150-170). Rest 10 minutes before slicing. Use pan juices as a moistening/seasoning sauce at the table. (Juices may be salty - taste.)
