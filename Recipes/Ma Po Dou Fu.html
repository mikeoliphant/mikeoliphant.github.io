Ma Po Dou Fu


Chile Bean Paste: Leekum Kee (Toban Djan).

12 ounces Tofu (firm in roomtemp box)
6 green onions (or more)
1/4-1/3 cup oil
6 ounces ground beef
1 1/2 tbsp sichuanese chili bean paste
1 tbsp fermented black beans
1 1/2 t kashmir paprika (spicy) or 3/4 t chile paste
1 cup chicken stock
1 teaspoon sugar
2 teaspoons soy sauce
1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
2 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 3 tbsp water
1/2 t ground roasted sichuan pepper (optional)


Cut the tofu into 3/4 inch cubes and steep in very hot, lightly salted water. Thinly slice the green onions at a steep angle (horse ears).

Add the oil to a wok over high heat until smoking. Add the beef and stir-fry until crumbly, a little crispy and brown, but not yet dry.

Turn heat to medium and add the chili bean paste. Stir-fry about 30 seconds. Add the black beans and paprika; stir-fry another 30 seconds.

Pour in the stock and mix gently, but thoroughly. Season with the sugar, soy sauce and salt. Simmer about 5 minutes, then taste for seasoning.

Stir in the green onions and some of the cornstarch mixture. Cook a minute or so. Use only as much cornstarch as is needed to create a slightly thickened, glossy sauce. Sprinkle the sichuan pepper over the top (if using) and serve.
