Salmon with Taragon Mushroom Sauce


Often served with plain boiled potatoes. Very good served over Jasmine Rice. Original used creme fraiche instead of sour cream or cream. If using fresh cream, add more vinegar. Adjust to taste accordingly. Any type of mushrooms work well.

2 tablespoons butter, divided
1 shallot, finely chopped
6 ounces mushrooms, sliced thickly
3/4 cup chicken stock
2 teaspoons cornstarch
1/2 teaspoon Dijon mustard
1/4 cup sour cream or fresh cream
3 tablespoons fresh tarragon, chopped or 1 tablespoon dried
1 teaspoon white wine vinegar
salt and cayenne pepper
Salmon


Cook salmon gentlely in butter; season with salt and cayenne. Keep warm. Mix the cornstarch, dijon mustard with a tablespoon of water; set aside.
Fry shallot in butter to soften. Add mushrooms and cook until they start to become juicy. Add (dried tarragon and) stock. Simmer a few minutes, then add the cream, (fresh tarragon,) vinegar and salt to taste. Mix in the cornstarch-mustard slurry and cook about a minute to thicken. Spoon sauce over the salmon to serve.
