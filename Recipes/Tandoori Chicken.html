Tandoori Chicken




2 tablespoons oil
1 teaspoon butter (or ghee)
1 t kashmiri chili powder
1/4 t hot chili poder
1 1/2 tbsp yogurt
1 1/2 t garlic paste
1 1/2 t ginger paste
1/8 t black salt
1/3 teaspoon black pepper
1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
1/2 teaspoon garam masala
1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
1 1/4 teaspoons kosher salt
2 t lemon or lime juice
1/8 teaspoon sugar
5 chicken thighs, skinless
2 teaspoons gram flour


Cut 2 or 3 slits in chicken. Mix chicken with everything except gram flour. Once well coated, sprinkle on gram flour and mix again. Let marinate at least 5 hours. Bring to RT before cooking (~1 hour). Cook about 20 minutes, turning often, over a Hot grill.
