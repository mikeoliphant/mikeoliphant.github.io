Pad Thai with Shrimp




4 garlic cloves, minced
1 tablespoon dried shrimp, chopped
2+ tbsp tamarind water
30 grams palm sugar
1 tablespoon soy sauce
1 tablespoon fish sauce
chilies and/or sauce/oil (eg fresh sambal)
1 t msg (aka magic powder)
3 green onions
6 ounces dried rice noodles, cooked and drained
4 ounces shrimp
5 oz chicken, small pieces
2-3 eggs
1 lg shallot, chopped
4 ounces bean sprouts


mix tamarind, sugar, soy, fish and chili sauce
oil + garlic + shrimp - cook and remove
eggs - cook and remove
chicken + garlic + shallot (+ fresh chili if using) - cook
add dried shrimp and noodles, cook 1 minute
add bean sprouts, green onions, sauces and msg - cook 30 seconds
return shrimp and eggs, mix
taste, adjust seasoning with fish sauce, salt and/or sugar
finish with cilantro
