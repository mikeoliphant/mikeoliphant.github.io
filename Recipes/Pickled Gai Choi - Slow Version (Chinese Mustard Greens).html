Pickled Gai Choi - Slow Version (Chinese Mustard Greens)




600 grams mustard greens, washed
140 g sticky rice (2/3 c)
1 liter (4 cups) water
50 grams (3 tablespoons) sea salt
67 grams (1/3 cup) white vinegar
Crushed garlic (optional)


Soak the rice in the water for half an hour. Drain, reserving the cloudy water.
Cut the thick part of the mustard greens into 1/4 inch slices; cut the leaves into strips about 1 x 4 inches. Place into a bowl and rub with 50 grams sea salt. Add the greens and garlic to a large jar. Add the vinegar to the jar, then enough rice water to cover the greens.
Tie the soaked rice into a cheesecloth sack and place on top of the greens to keep them submerged. Let ferment at room temp for 4-6 days or until pickled to your liking. Store in the refridgerator up to 6 weeks.
